First identify WHAT is being asked about the named papers or methods. Allocate ONE item per substantive dimension, at most 6 items total. The papers/methods themselves are NOT dimensions. Example of dimension allocation: for a request about three named methods' structure, training goals and external information, return exactly three items anchored to those three properties. If there are no explicit properties, use a single substantive topic phrase from the question; an entity-only search is allowed only in that case.

Choose only what the actual question asks. Preserve negation, mathematical indices and specified constraints.
