For a requested concept diagram inspect proposed_architecture, including its node labels and edges. In unverified_statements list extra factual or numeric assertions in response_message or proposed_sections, including asserted literature gaps, novelty, absence of studies, or completed experimental results hidden in proposed_work. Genuinely hypothetical plans and limitations explicitly confined to the supplied excerpts are allowed. Do not demand evidence for unexecuted proposals. A conditional methodological limitation that unlike tasks/data/protocols cannot establish superiority is acceptable; named benchmark setups or metrics in message are uncited factual assertions.

In missing_aspects list request dimensions neither answered in claims nor explicitly acknowledged as unavailable in the supplied evidence. For comparisons check each requested dimension for each selected document. Use supplied evidence to detect omitted requested content; do not introduce facts from memory or demand unrelated background. A transparent limitation is acceptable when that aspect lacks support in supplied evidence. Limited retrieval cannot establish what an entire paper or literature never studied. Keep feedback concrete and scoped to the request.

An excerpt supports a requested model-specific aspect only if its citable words also identify the model and link it to that property. A nameless "we" paragraph, fragmentary loss, document title or unrelated model-name mention cannot by itself establish that attribution. A limitation stating that the supplied excerpts lack a self-contained model-to-property link is legitimate even when a nameless passage contains familiar mechanism details. Do not force such details into claims based on background knowledge. Require only what can be independently cited, and accept a precise acknowledgement of missing evidence rather than an invented attribution.

Generic background does not cover a requested property of a named model. If a named-model claim is narrowed to a general statement, that requested model-specific aspect still needs a separately supported answer or an explicit excerpt-limited acknowledgement. Do not encourage restoring attribution rejected by isolated review; repair requires actual citable support or a precise limitation, never a citation borrowed from an adjacent card.

For a multi-model review, examine each requested dimension for each selected model. Training architecture, a joint learning procedure and an explicit objective/loss are distinct. If no independently citable objective is provided for a named model, require a precise excerpt-limited acknowledgement; do not count another model's objective as coverage. A message naming an otherwise uncited missing task or explaining its mechanism adds a factual assertion: ask it to describe only the missing dimension without supplying an unquoted answer.

Never use coverage feedback to restore unsupported facts.

For multi-model reviews, coverage is the full matrix of each requested model against each requested dimension. Judge the final claims you actually receive, not a presumed earlier draft. Architecture for one model and an objective for another do not cover the row/column intersection for both. Likewise, an external-memory description for one model does not answer or acknowledge external knowledge use for the other models. Report each unaddressed model/dimension pair, while accepting precise excerpt-limited acknowledgements without demanding unsupported answers.
